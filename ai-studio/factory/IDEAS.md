# Game Factory — ideas

The Designer takes from **Queue** top to bottom and invents new pitches when it runs dry. Daniel can add lines to Queue at any time.

## Queue
- Gravity Golf — one-touch golf where the ball curves around planets' gravity wells; par per hole (physics / puzzle)
- Rail Switch — trains of different colours roll across a track network; flip switches so each reaches its matching station (puzzle-action)
- Hex Sweeper — minesweeper on a hex grid, with chording, a safe first click and a timer (logic)
- Lantern Run — endless runner in the dark; your light radius shrinks, so grab oil to see further (runner)
- Word Forge — letters fall into a grid; tap adjacent tiles to form words; longer words make bigger chains (word)
- Orbital Defense — tower defense on a ring around a planet; enemies spiral in (tower defense)
- Echo Tap — rhythm game where the pattern plays once and you repeat it, getting longer and faster (rhythm / memory)
- Tide Pools — tiny ecosystem: place algae, shrimp and crabs to keep every species alive for 3 minutes (sim / goal)
- Ricochet — one shot per level; bank the bullet off walls to hit every target (aim puzzle)
- Picross Daily — nonogram puzzles generated from a seed, with a daily puzzle and a mistake counter (logic)
