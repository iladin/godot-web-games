Bugs:
- [P1] The world simulates behind the title screen, so an idle title screen eventually loses the game — repro: launch and do not click (probe p2: main.tscn with no input, headless --fixed-fps 60) — expected: the sim is frozen until "launch"; actual: `main._process` calls `u.step()` whenever `state != OVER`, TITLE included. After 60 s on the title, u.time=60 and raid_t=140. After 360 s, 1 raid has launched and credits went 30000→40137. At u.time=1653 s (27.5 min) the HQ is destroyed and the game jumps to "HQ LOST" (state=2) without the player ever starting. Real players lose 1–3 min of the 200 s raid countdown just reading the controls, and the win time bonus (3600−time) is charged for title time. Related cosmetic issue: the flight HUD, "F: Dock at Sovereign HQ" and "Click to capture mouse for steering" render under the title text (`.tools/shots/void-sovereign/title.png`). Fix hint: skip `u.step` unless state == PLAYING, or build the world on start.
- [P1] Infinite-credit arbitrage at any Trade Hub — repro: dock at the Haven's Reach Trade Hub, press Buy 10 on Munitions four times, then Sell all, and repeat (probe p4 calls `u.transact` the same way the dock buttons do) — expected: a round trip at one station loses money or breaks even; actual: `transact()` prices the whole batch at the pre-trade price, and the Trade Hub both buys and sells every ware. Each buy raises the price, so selling back is always profitable. 50 cycles gave munitions +11,333 cr and alloy +5,333 cr, with stock back at 200 and no time needed. Even a single Buy 10 → Sell all nets about +23 cr. This trivialises the whole economy loop (ships and stations become free with clicking). Fix hint: price per unit along the curve (or average of start and end fill), and/or a buy/sell spread.
- [P1] When the run ends while the command map is open, clicking or tapping does not restart — repro: start, open the map (M or touch MAP), let the HQ die or the last Nest burn (win is usually triggered from the map, by sending the fleet). Probe p1 `--case=overmap` calls `_on_finished(false)` with mode=map — expected: "Click, tap or press Space to play again" works; actual: MapView (`mouse_filter = STOP`, full rect) stays visible under the end overlay and eats the click and the emulated touch click. After clicking at (400,400), clicking at (1100,650) and tapping at (400,400), state stays 2 (OVER). Only Space restarts. On a phone the only escape is the "Close map (M)" button, which sits below the fold of the side panel at 1280x720 and under the 65% black overlay, so this is effectively a softlock on touch. Map buttons (buy/build/orders) also stay clickable on the end screen. The same thing in dock mode is milder: clicks on the panel are eaten, clicks outside it restart. Fix hint: in `_on_finished`, `_set_mode("flight")` or hide map/dock.
- [P2] Tutorial tip 3 ("Press R / Tap TGT to lock onto a Hive ship") cannot be completed in the home sector and blocks tips 4–6 — repro: start, steer and throttle (tips 1–2 latch), press R in Haven's Reach (probe p6) — expected: the tip can be done where it appears, or it is skipped after a while; actual: Haven's Reach has 0 Hive ships for at least the first 270 s of game time (sampled every 30 s), and R does nothing. The tip stays "TIP 3/6" and the dock/map/land tips never show, because `current()` returns the first unfinished step. New players get stuck on a tip that tells them to do something impossible. Fix hint: make "target" conditional on a Hive ship being present (fall back to the next step), or let it time out.
- [P3] Stations can be built anywhere, including inside a planet — repro: probe p8 calls `u.build_station("solar", hq.sector, sector.planet_pos)` (the map placement click calls the same function) — expected: placement rejected inside the planet radius or on another station; actual: it succeeds, with the station at distance 0 from the planet centre (PLANET_RADIUS 1500), and 28,000 cr is taken. A Solar Array inside Ember cannot be docked or visited. Fix hint: validate in `build_station` against the planet radius and the radii of existing stations.
