Bugs:
- [P2] The surface stays live under the end screen
  - Repro: probe p1/p1b. Land on Ember, then call `flow.finish(false)` (the same path as the HQ dying or the last Nest burning while you are on foot).
  - Expected: the world freezes and one click restarts.
  - Actual: `close_overlays()` keeps mode=surface, and `main._process` keeps calling `surface.tick`. `fps.input_enabled` stays true:
    - W moved the player 17 m in 2 s on the "HQ LOST" screen.
    - Foes and waves keep running.
    - If the player then dies, `_leave_surface(true)` fires after game over. The HUD shows "Emergency evacuation to orbit — hull damaged", the player ship loses hull (600→360), and the mode flips to flight under the end overlay.
    - The first desktop click is eaten by `fps_player._unhandled_input` to capture the mouse (state stays OVER, mouse_mode=2). Only the second click restarts.
  - This is the root cause of the parking-lot P3 "surface game-over needs a double click".
  - Fix hint: in `close_overlays`, set `surface.fps.input_enabled = false` and skip `surface.tick` unless state == PLAYING (or leave the surface on finish).
- [P2] The raid deterrent counts any Defense Platform anywhere in the target's sector, even one buried inside a planet
  - Repro: probe p2 at u.time=1300 in Haven's Reach.
    - A platform at (30000, 0, 30000), 42 km from the HQ and far outside its 1700 m range, drops the HQ go-chance from 1.0 to 0.25.
    - A 2nd platform built at `sectors[0].planet_pos` (inside Ember, which `build_station` accepts; see the next bug) drops it to 0.0625.
    - A 3rd platform stacked on the 1st drops it to 0.0156.
  - Expected: only platforms that actually defend the target deter a raid.
  - Actual: `Director.raid_go_chance` counts every alive player "defense" station in `ents_in(target.sector)`, with no range check.
  - Impact (probe p3, sim-only, HQ made invulnerable to isolate raid decisions, t=1200–2100 s):
    - 0 platforms: 11 player raids, 0 turned back.
    - 1 platform: 4 player raids, 4 turned back.
    - 2 platforms: 1 player raid, 8 turned back.
  - Cancelled raids vanish rather than redirecting. For 60k cr the player becomes nearly raid-immune, and the platforms don't need to be anywhere useful, which strengthens the S0011 "turtle" concern.
  - Fix hint: count only platforms within `range` (or ~2x range) of the target, and fold this into the planned deterrent tuning task.
